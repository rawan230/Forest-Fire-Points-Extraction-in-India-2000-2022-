## 2. Corrections

- **National forest cover.** The `forest_cover_pct` column of `extraction_summary.csv` (9.86–10.43%) is computed over the rectangular LULC download subset, which includes neighbouring countries and ocean. Over India only, forest cover is **18.27–19.32%**.
- **Rasterising these points onto the 1/120° NDVI grid.** The rule used downstream, `row = round((lat − f)/e)`, rounds against the top-left *edge* and puts 74.9% of points in a neighbouring pixel. Use `floor` (the containing pixel). The forest filter in this repository is **correct**, because it rounds against pixel *centres* taken from the LCCS NetCDF coordinate arrays.
- **Points not on valid NDVI pixels:** 412 fall outside the India raster mask and 528 fall on NaN NDVI pixels. These are dropped silently downstream.
- **Burned-area validation scripts are tracked in this repository** (commit 5b2c316). Earlier documentation said they were not preserved.
